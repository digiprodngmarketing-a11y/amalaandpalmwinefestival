More than a meal

A whole culture
around one table.

Amala and palmwine are the starting point. The festival opens the door to the sounds, style, stories, creativity and hospitality of Southwest Nigeria — bringing generations and communities together.

Expect a lively cultural playground where food meets music, indigenous fashion, games, storytelling and new connections.

FlavourAmala, abula, palmwine and regional food culture.
RhythmLive music, traditional sounds and entertainment.
HeritageFashion, stories, games and community.
