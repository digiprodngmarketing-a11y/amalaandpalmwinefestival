More than a meal

A table set for
our culture.

The Amala and Palmwine Festival brings people together through the food we love and the traditions we share. It is a space to celebrate Nigerian culinary heritage, discover local talent, enjoy live entertainment and create memories together.

Come hungry for good food, good music and the unmistakable warmth of community.

Our heritage is best celebrated together — one plate, one song and one shared story at a time.
